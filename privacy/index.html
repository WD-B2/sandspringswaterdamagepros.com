Privacy Policy.

How this website and our phone line handle information.

What we collect.

This website does not use forms. When you call (918) 306-8113, we receive your phone number and the details you choose to share about your water damage. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information.

We use call information to respond to your request, to dispatch a crew and to improve service. We do not sell your personal information.

Website data.

Our hosting provider may collect basic technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and performance. Embedded maps load from Google and may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

Your choices.

You may ask us not to contact you again, and you may ask what information we hold about your call, and you can reach us by phone at the number on this site.

Changes.

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
