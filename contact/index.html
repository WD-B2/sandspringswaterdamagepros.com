Call the 24/7 line.

The fastest way to reach us is by phone. We do not use web forms, because water damage cannot wait for an email reply. Call (918) 306-8113 at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays.

What to tell the dispatcher.

  1. Your address and the nearest cross street.
  2. What the source is and whether it is stopped.
  3. Which rooms are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  4. Whether water touches outlets, the panel or appliances.
  5. Whether you have started to contact your insurer.
