Tulsa permits and who handles what.

The City of Tulsa says ordinary repairs are generally exempt from a permit, while cutting into walls or altering piping or wiring may need one. Water damage repair often involves exactly that kind of work. Ask your contractor who pulls the permit before demolition starts.

A plumber repairs the pipe, an electrician checks wet wiring and the restoration crew removes water and dries the structure. Splitting the jobs up this way keeps the scope clear and each price separate.

River, levee and storm water on the Tulsa side.

The Arkansas River runs along the south and west edge of Tulsa. A levee of roughly 20 miles between Sand Springs and Tulsa protects about 10,000 people, and it is owned by Tulsa County Drainage District 12. Levees reduce risk but do not remove it, and floodplain questions for a specific address go to the city and the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood, so many Tulsa homeowners ask their agent about separate flood coverage before storm season. If water reached ground level from outside, the flood damage cleanup page explains the response.

What to tell the dispatcher in Tulsa.

Give the cross streets, whether the building is a house, apartment or commercial space and what floor the water is on. Water on an upper floor travels down, so tell the dispatcher if ceilings below are wet. If you are in a multi-unit building, mention whether the neighbors below are affected and whether the landlord has been told.

For apartments and rental houses, the landlord or property manager often has to approve the work and notify their insurer. Photograph the damage and send the images to them right away. Tenants should also ask their renter's insurance about personal belongings.

Insurance and records for Tulsa properties.

Tulsa homes and businesses file claims under the same rules as everywhere in Oklahoma. The Oklahoma Insurance Department says standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood, interior stormwater without roof or wall damage and backed-up drains or sewers. Sudden pipe and appliance failures are commonly covered.

Keep moisture readings, photographs and invoices together in one folder. If the loss is in a rental, share the same file with the landlord. Clear records shorten the time between the first call and the final repair.
