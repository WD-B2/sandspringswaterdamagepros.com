Find the page that matches your water.

Water damage is not one problem. A burst pipe, a failing sump pump and a flooded creek all put water in the house, but each one needs a different plan. This index groups every service by cause, so you can find the page that matches what you see.

If you are not sure which group fits, call the 24/7 line and describe what happened. The dispatcher will point you to the right plan, and the scope and price are explained before work starts. Call now if water is still spreading.

Pipes and plumbing.

Failures in supply lines, tanks and lines under the foundation.

  • Burst Pipe Water DamageA split supply line in a wall, ceiling or floor, with the main valve closed first..
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamagePipes that froze, cracked and flooded as they thawed..
  • Water Heater Leak CleanupA tank or fitting that failed and wet the floor and walls around it..
  • Slab Leak Water DamageA pipe leaking under or inside the concrete slab..

Appliances and HVAC.

Equipment that leaks into floors, cabinets and ceilings.

  • Appliance Leak Water DamageWasher hoses, dishwashers, ice makers and softeners..
  • AC Leak Water DamageCondensate lines and drain pans that overflowed into ceilings and closets..
  • Kitchen Water DamageCabinets, countertops and floors wet from sinks and appliances..

Weather and building envelope.

Water that came from outside the building.

  • Ceiling Water Damage RepairStains, sagging drywall and roof-leak water coming through the ceiling..
  • Storm Water DamageWind-driven rain, hail damage and storm openings in roofs and walls..
  • Flood Damage CleanupRising water from storms, creeks and the river..
  • Basement Water DamageSump failures, seepage and leaks in the room below the house..
  • Crawl Space Water DamageStanding water and wet insulation under the house..

Water removal and drying.

The core steps used on most jobs.

  • Water ExtractionPumps and extractors that remove standing water fast..
  • Structural DryingMoisture mapping, air movers and dehumidifiers for walls and floors..
  • Sewage Backup CleanupContaminated water from drains and toilets, removed and sanitized..

Floors and walls.

Materials that need their own save or replace decision.

  • Hardwood Floor Water DamageCupping, crowning and buckling boards after a leak..
  • Carpet Water DamageWet carpet and pad, and the save or replace decision..
  • Wall and Drywall Water DamageStained, bubbling or soft walls and wet insulation..

Commercial.

Buildings with more than one tenant, room or owner.

  • Commercial Water DamageShops, offices and multi-unit buildings with water damage..

How the services fit together.

Most jobs combine several of these services. A burst pipe may need extraction, drying and a drywall repair. A storm leak may need ceiling repair, structural drying and carpet work. The crew builds one plan across them and explains each part before it starts.

What to do before you call.

  • Stop the source if you can do it safely.
  • Stay out of water near outlets and the electrical panel.
  • Photograph the damage and write down the time you found it.
  • Have your address and a callback number ready.

The standard behind professional water damage work is ANSI/IICRC S500, and each service page explains how its ideas apply to that cause.
