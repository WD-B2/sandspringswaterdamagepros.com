How to Shut Off Your Home Water Main in Sand Springs, OK.

Where the valve usually is, how to turn it, how to test it before an emergency and what to do when it is stuck.

Written by the Sand Springs Water Damage Pros team. Published 2026.

24/7 emergency line.
(918) 306-8113.
Call now.

The most valuable thing you can know about your plumbing is where the shutoff valve is. When a pipe bursts, every minute counts, and the person who can stop the water in thirty seconds saves thousands of gallons. Most homeowners cannot find it under stress, so find it today.

The two kinds of shutoff.

There is a fixture valve, which stops water to one sink, toilet or appliance, and there is the main valve, which stops water to the whole house. Learn both. A small leak is fixed with a fixture valve, while a burst line needs the main.

Where to look for the main.

Common main shutoff locations.
Location.What to look for.
Near the street meter boxA concrete or plastic lid, often marked water.
Where the line enters the houseA valve on the wall near the front foundation.
Utility room or garageNear the water heater or softener.
Basement or crawl spaceOn the wall closest to the street.
Inside a closetBehind an access panel in some homes.

Newer homes often have a valve inside near the water heater. Older homes may have only the curb stop at the meter. If your meter is in a box at the street, a special key may be needed, so ask your provider.

Ball valves and gate valves.

A ball valve has a lever handle. A quarter turn so the handle sits across the pipe closes it. A gate valve has a round wheel handle and takes several turns clockwise to close. Gate valves can stick or break after years of disuse, so work them gently.

Turn it off and test.

  1. Close the valve fully.
  2. Open a faucet at the lowest point in the house to drain the line.
  3. Open a faucet at the highest point to let air in.
  4. Check that water stops flowing.
  5. Tag the valve with a bright label so everyone in the house knows.

Exercise the valve once a year so it does not seize. If it leaks at the stem or will not turn, call a plumber to replace it before you need it.

What to do if the valve is stuck or broken.

Do not force a gate valve with a wrench. If the main will not turn, shut off the supply at the meter if you have the key, or call your water provider. Turn off the water heater to protect it from running dry. Then call a plumber and a restoration crew.

Sand Springs water utility context.

The city water system serves about 12,000 customers over roughly 150 square miles, and about 4,500 customers live outside the city limits. After the water is off.

Once the water is off, stay out of wet areas near outlets and the panel. Take photos and start extraction. The burst pipe page explains the next steps, and the water extraction page covers what a crew does. Call (918) 306-8113 for the 24/7 line.

People in Sperry and Mannford may be on different providers, so check your bill. Put a flashlight and a wrench near the valve and write the location on the inside of a cabinet.

Fixture valves you should know.

Every toilet, sink and appliance has its own small valve. Walk through the house once and touch each of them. Turn them a quarter turn and back to see that they move. A valve that is frozen with mineral buildup will not help in a crisis, and replacing one costs far less than a flooded room.

  • Toilets: a small oval handle on the wall or floor behind the tank.
  • Sinks: two valves under the cabinet, one for hot and one for cold.
  • Washing machine: two valves on the wall behind it.
  • Dishwasher: usually under the kitchen sink.
  • Water heater: a cold supply valve on top of the tank.

Teach everyone in the house.

The person who knows where the valve is might not be home when a pipe breaks. Show every adult and older child where it is and how to turn it. Take a photo of the valve and keep it in a shared album. For rental properties, put the location on a card near the front door.
