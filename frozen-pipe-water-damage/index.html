Frozen pipe or burst pipe: what to do now.

Dispatch runs 24/7, nights, weekends and holidays included, so call right away. If a pipe has split, shut off the main water valve and open a faucet to drain the line. Do not use an open flame on a frozen pipe, and do not touch outlets or cords in wet areas.

If a pipe is frozen but not yet leaking, open the faucet and gently warm the area with a hair dryer or space heater placed safely away from water. Turn the water off if you see any cracks.

Why the water appears when the ice melts.

Ice expands, and a pipe can split without leaking while it is frozen. The leak starts when the ice thaws. That is why the flood often comes hours after the cold snap ends, when no one is expecting it.

Tulsa-area winters bring sudden drops below freezing after mild days, and unheated areas such as garages, crawl spaces, attics and exterior walls are where pipes usually fail. The National Weather Service Tulsa office publishes forecasts before freezing events.

Where pipes freeze in a house.

Exterior walls.

Pipes close to the outside surface with little insulation.

Crawl space and garage.

Unheated spaces where lines are exposed.

Attic.

Supply lines run through cold air above insulation.

Outdoor spigots.

Hose bibs that were not drained or covered.

Insurance and frozen pipes.

Read before you file.

The Oklahoma Insurance Department notes that frozen pipe damage may not be covered if the home was left unheated and the damage resulted from negligence. Keep the heat on and document steps taken to prevent freezing.

Preventing the next freeze.

Most freeze breaks are predictable. Pipes on exterior walls, in unheated garages and in crawl spaces are the usual victims. Insulating those lines with foam sleeves, sealing air leaks around where pipes enter the house and keeping garage doors closed during cold snaps make a real difference.

On the coldest nights, let a faucet drip slightly and open the cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls. A small flow of water through the line makes freezing less likely, and the warm room air reaches the pipes.

If you are away when it freezes.

Vacant houses are the most exposed. If you travel in winter, keep the thermostat at a safe level, ask someone to check the house and consider shutting off the main water and draining the lines. Remember that insurers may treat damage from an unheated house as neglect, so heat and documentation matter.

According to the Oklahoma Insurance Department, frozen pipe damage may not be covered when it results from negligence such as leaving a home unheated.
